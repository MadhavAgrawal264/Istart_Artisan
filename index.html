Help & Support

Simple guides to help you list crafts, set fair prices, and talk to buyers.

1. Adding a New Craft

Go to AI Cataloging to upload a photo and let AI suggest a title and story, or add manually in My Products. You can save as a Draft first or Publish right away.

2. Setting Your Price

Use Fair Pricing to add up your materials, labour time, packaging, and other expenses plus profit margin — then apply that price straight to your product.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can see my draft products?

Only you! Draft products stay private in your dashboard until you click Publish.

What if the AI photo tool does not work?

If your internet is slow or AI is busy, you can always fill in or edit your product name, materials, and description yourself in plain words.

How do buyers contact me?

Every question from a buyer shows up in Buyer Enquiries and your top notification bell, where you can reply and update the status.
